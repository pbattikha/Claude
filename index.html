Our Educational Approach

The STEAM Advantage

Around 75% of the fastest growing industries require STEAM skills. We prepare your child for the future through purposefully selected play experiences.

Science

Exploring the natural world through experiments, observations, and discovery-based learning.

  • Nature exploration
  • Simple experiments
  • Sensory activities

Technology

Age-appropriate introduction to coding, robotics, and digital tools through the ELSA program.

  • Basic coding
  • Robotics play
  • Interactive apps

Engineering

Building, constructing, and problem-solving activities that develop spatial awareness.

  • Building blocks
  • Construction play
  • Problem solving

Arts

Creative expression through various mediums including painting, music, and drama.

  • Visual arts
  • Music & movement
  • Drama & stories

Mathematics

Foundational numeracy concepts through hands-on activities and real-world applications.

  • Counting & numbers
  • Patterns & shapes
  • Measurement

Early Learning STEM Australia (ELSA) Program

In our preschool room, we bring the ELSA program to life with digital and hands-on STEM experiences using interactive apps including Patterns & Relationships, Investigation, Location & Arrangement, and Representation.

A Day at iSTEAM AHEAD

What Happens Every Day

Our daily routines blend structured learning with play-based discovery, ensuring every moment supports your child's development.

7:30 - 8:30am

Arrival & Welcome Circle

Settling routines, morning greeting, and community meeting where we discuss the day's activities and celebrate special moments.

8:30 - 10:30am

Focused Learning Time

STEAM-focused activities including coding, building, experiments, art projects, and skill-building games tailored to each room's age group.

10:30 - 11:00am

Snack & Outdoor Play

Healthy snack time followed by active outdoor exploration, physical development, and free play in our purpose-built outdoor spaces.

11:00am - 12:30pm

Creative Play & Exploration

Child-led play in learning centres, creative stations, music, dramatic play, and social skill development through collaborative activities.

12:30 - 2:00pm

Lunch & Rest Time

Nutritious home-prepared lunch, quiet time, naps for younger children, and story time with engaging books and activities.

2:00 - 4:00pm

Afternoon Discovery & Afternoon Activities

Nature exploration, garden maintenance, sensory experiences, and second structured learning time focused on engagement and discovery.

4:00 - 6:00pm

Free Play & Pickup

Relaxed afternoon play, quiet activities, snack time, and smooth transitions as families arrive for pickup with daily updates.
