Welcome to iSTEAM AHEAD

Nurturing Tomorrow's Innovators

Founded in 2019, we are one of the few centres in Australia offering a unique STEAM programme to early years learning, giving your child skills to become problem solvers and communicators of the 21st century.

5+ Years of Excellence

A Home Away From Home

At iSTEAM AHEAD, we believe every child is naturally curious and capable. Our purpose-built 79-place centre in the heart of Brookvale provides a safe, nurturing environment where children aged 6 weeks to 5 years can explore, discover, and grow.

Qualified Educators

Led by experienced primary school teacher Moira Battikha with expertise in reading recovery and special needs education.

STEAM Methodology

Our unique approach integrates Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts & Mathematics into everyday play-based learning.

Individual Attention

Every child's routines are reflected in our daily program, ensuring a strong sense of wellbeing and belonging.

"STEAM is a mindset approach - it's about creating children to be thinkers, problem solvers, creative minds who ask questions and are inquisitive."

- Moira Battikha, Director
Our Educational Approach

The STEAM Advantage

Around 75% of the fastest growing industries require STEAM skills. We prepare your child for the future through purposefully selected play experiences that invite them to new learning adventures.

Science

Exploring the natural world through experiments, observations, and discovery-based learning that sparks wonder and curiosity.

  • Nature exploration
  • Simple experiments
  • Sensory activities

Technology

Age-appropriate introduction to coding, robotics, and digital tools through the ELSA program and interactive learning apps.

  • Basic coding concepts
  • Robotics play
  • Interactive apps

Engineering

Building, constructing, and problem-solving activities that develop spatial awareness and logical thinking skills.

  • Building blocks
  • Construction play
  • Problem solving

Arts

Creative expression through various mediums including painting, music, drama, and imaginative play.

  • Visual arts
  • Music & movement
  • Drama & storytelling

Mathematics

Foundational numeracy concepts through hands-on activities, patterns, shapes, and real-world applications.

  • Counting & numbers
  • Patterns & shapes
  • Measurement

Early Learning STEM Australia (ELSA) Program

In our preschool room, we bring the ELSA program to life, offering children a blend of digital and hands-on STEM experiences using interactive apps including Patterns & Relationships, Investigation, Location & Arrangement, and Representation.
